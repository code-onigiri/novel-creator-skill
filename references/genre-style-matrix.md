# Genre-Style Mapping Matrix

| Genre | Recommended POV | Sentence Length & Rhythm | Dialogue Ratio | Narrative Focus | Style Keywords |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Xuanhuan / Level-Up | Third-person close | Medium-short sentences, strong beat | 35%-50% | Growth, face-slapping, level-up feedback | Dense payoffs, staged climaxes |
| Urban Supernatural | First/Third person mixed | Short sentences, fast rhythm | 40%-60% | Realistic pressure and reversals | Colloquial, quick reactions |
| Historical Political Intrigue | Third-person | Medium-long sentences, slow start fast finish | 25%-40% | Information asymmetry, game chains | Restrained, subplot-driven |
| Mystery & Deduction | First-person / Limited third | Short sentences + white space | 20%-35% | Clues, misdirection, payoff | Calm, precise, reversals |
| Sci-Fi Adventure | Third-person | Medium sentences, steady | 20%-35% | Setting payoff, cost of action | Concrete, hard logic |
| Romance / Women's Fiction | First/Third person | Medium sentences, emotional peaks | 45%-65% | Relationship evolution, emotional progression | Delicate, emotional pull |
| Horror & Thriller | First-person preferred | Short sentences, fragmented rhythm | 15%-30% | Sensory pressure, escalation of the unknown | Oppressive atmosphere, suspense |

## Selection Rules
1. First, select the baseline by genre.
2. Then adjust sentence length and information density by target reader age.
3. If you have sample chapters, prioritize their style features; the genre baseline serves only as a boundary constraint.
4. When multiple candidate styles appear for the same genre, prioritize the one with "clearer conflict expression".
